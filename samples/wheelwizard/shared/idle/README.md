# Shared standing idles

All four clips are loops at 60 fps. Their first and last poses match, their feet remain grounded, and they preserve the Mii's own face apart from brief expressions. The two original files are preserved as supplied.

| File | Loop length | Character |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Idle_breathing.miianim` | 6.00 s | Original quiet breathing and occasional blink. |
| `Idle_weight_shift.miianim` | 7.00 s | Original relaxed weight shift and side glance. |
| `Idle_curious_look.miianim` | 6.00 s | Calm pauses, two unhurried side glances, soft breaths, and quick blinks. |
| `Idle_tiny_groove.miianim` | 4.00 s | A restrained in-place groove with knee bounce and alternating shoulder/hand accents. |

Use breathing or curious look as a quiet default. Weight shift adds a little movement, and tiny groove gives a more playful screen some personality. The new loops include a short neutral hold at the seam and have no `done` event or particles. They can follow a shared one-shot without a pose jump.
