# Shared error reactions

Both are one-shots at 60 fps. They start and finish in neutral standing, keep their feet planted, and restore the Mii's normal face. Return to idle on `done`.

| File | Duration | Motion | Useful events |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Error_puzzled_shrug.miianim` | 2.50 s | A confused glance, clear palms-up shrug, and relaxed recovery. | `shrug` 42, `done` 150 |
| `Error_startled_recovery.miianim` | 2.30 s | A small startled squash, two disbelieving head shakes, and regained composure. | `startled` 8, `recovered` 104, `done` 138 |

The shrug suits a missing choice or unavailable action. The startled reaction is more expressive for a failed operation. Neither loops, moves the character off its standing spot, or requires effects.
